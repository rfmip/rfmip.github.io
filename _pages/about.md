    <h1>Radiative Forcing Model Intercomparison Project</h1>
    <p>The Radiative Forcing Model Intercomparison Project (RFMIP) is a model intercomparison project designed to diagnose effective radiative forcing and evaluate its uncertainty in Global Climate Models.</p>

    <div class="rfmip-hero-actions">

As part of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project phase 7 (CMIP7), the second iteration of the Radiative Forcing Model Intercomparison Project (RFMIP2.0) consists of a series of fixed-Sea Surface Temperature simulations aimed at addressing the following questions:

<div class="rfmip-q-grid">
  <div class="rfmip-q">
    <div class="rfmip-q-num">01</div>
    <div>
      <h3>Present-day forcing</h3>
      <p>What is the present-day radiative forcing, and its key anthropogenic contributors, since pre-industrial times?</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="rfmip-q">
    <div class="rfmip-q-num">02</div>
    <div>
      <h3>Temporal evolution</h3>
      <p>What is the temporal evolution of the radiative forcing, and its components, over the historical period and into the future?</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="rfmip-q">
    <div class="rfmip-q-num">03</div>
    <div>
      <h3>Climate-state dependence</h3>
      <p>What is the influence of the underlying climate state on radiative forcing?</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="rfmip-q">
    <div class="rfmip-q-num">04</div>
    <div>
      <h3>Forcing vs. feedback</h3>
      <p>To what extent is radiative forcing separable from radiative feedbacks when considering land processes?</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
